# DHDK crash course - DH Research Tools & Literacy 
## 24/25 bibliography and links

This is a list of all the links and sources used for the DHDK Crash Course for DH Research Tools & Literacy in 2024 and 2025. They're grouped by theme, and include a mix of scholarly sources, articles, and practical tools. There is no consistent citation system in place, sorry if that's triggering. 

If you spot any errors please let me know, thank you! 🙏🏻
