# DHDK Crash Courses - Resources 

This repo holds the teaching resources for crash courses I've been holding for the [DHDK master's course](https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/DigitalHumanitiesKnowledge) at the University of Bologna, starting in 2023. 

These crash courses are offered to first-year students as a way to help them come to grip with the course's requirements, which can be quite extensive. Between 2023 and 2025,
I taught a new course titled 'Tools for DH Research & Literacy', inspired by my time on the course and seeing some of my younger colleagues struggle with what I took 
to be basic literacy when it came to finding information on the internet. Over three years, the course has evolved towards an introduction to research and data literacy, combined with a tour through the types of tools they might want to use for the projects the course requires. At the same time, the rise of GenAI and LLMs has made an impact on what research literacy means and the course is adapting as best as possible. 

Starting in 2026, the first-year courses are now offered half-way through the first year (instead of at the start) as a way to mitigate some of the lack of context students felt in previous year. At the same time I'm also taking on the DH Project Management crash course and testing out a second round of crash courses for the 2nd year students, as a way to revisit and discuss these ideas now that they have a better understanding of what they're doing and may want to do. 

The contents of the repo are held in branches (navigate to them via the dropdown above) where each branch is for a specific student cohort and academic year. Materials provided include slides and bibliographies.
